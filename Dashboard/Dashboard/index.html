About Smart Pet Feeder

Revolutionizing pet care in South Africa

Our Mission

To provide pet owners with intelligent, reliable, and convenient automated feeding solutions that ensure their pets are always well-fed and healthy.

Technology

Our Smart Pet Feeder uses advanced AI and computer vision to recognize your pet and dispense food automatically.

  • Real-time pet recognition
  • Automatic portion control
  • WiFi connectivity
  • Water quality monitoring

Safety & Quality

All our products meet international safety standards and are designed with your pet's well-being in mind.

  • CE, RoHS, FC Certified
  • BPA-free materials
  • Easy to clean design
  • Safe for all pets

Made in South Africa

Proudly designed and manufactured in Cape Town, South Africa, supporting local innovation and employment.

  • Local manufacturing
  • Supporting local economy
  • African innovation
  • Reliable support

Sustainability

We are committed to sustainable practices and eco-friendly manufacturing processes.

  • Energy-efficient design
  • Recyclable materials
  • Reduced food waste
  • Environmentally conscious

Our Story

Smart Pet Feeder was born from a simple idea: to make pet care easier and more reliable for pet owners. Founded in Cape Town, South Africa, we've grown from a small startup to a leading provider of smart pet solutions.

Our team of engineers, designers, and pet lovers work tirelessly to create products that make a real difference in the lives of pets and their owners. We believe that technology should serve to enhance the bond between humans and animals, not replace it.
